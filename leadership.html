Leadership & volunteering

Acquiring Competencies for Employment (ACE)

BYLC

  • A two-week professional-development programme from BYLC that helps university students prepare for employment.
  • What I did: completed the programme and received a certificate of participation. It covered:
  • Professional Writing: efficient English writing, and how to write professional emails, resumes and cover letters.
  • Communication & Public Speaking: digital communication skills, public-speaking practice and interview etiquette.
  • Leadership Training: core leadership concepts, conscious leadership and teamwork.

Bangladesh Youth Volunteer Award programme

Go Up Foundation and Amra Scout Group · Dhaka · 11 March 2023

  • Organised by Go Up Foundation with Amra Scout Group, a Dhaka scout group of Bangladesh Scouts that supports street children and runs relief work.
  • What I did: took part in the programme in Dhaka on 11 March 2023 and received a certificate of participation.

BD Clean Movement

Volunteer · 29 March 2019

  • BD Clean is a nationwide volunteer platform, founded in 2016, that cleans a chosen public place every week; it had more than 44,000 active members by 2023.
  • What I did: joined a BD Clean clean-up as a volunteer on 29 March 2019. Volunteers collect litter from the chosen public place and talk with people nearby about keeping it clean.
  • Recognised for contributing time, effort and dedication towards a cleaner and greener Bangladesh.

AIUB Computer Club

Certificate of Appreciation

  • A student technology club at AIUB, founded in 2012, that runs programming contests, workshops, CS Fest and the Cyber Gaming Fest.
  • What I did: active member of the club’s organising team, helping run these events:
  • Software Requirements Analysis & QA Seminars (2020–2022).
  • AIUB Cyber Gaming Fest (2018 and 2022): esports tournaments where students from many institutions compete in popular titles.
  • Samsung Esports Championship (2019): an esports event run in collaboration with Samsung.
  • JARVIS – Intra ACC Carnival (2021): the club’s internal carnival, with technical challenges, project showcases and prize-giving.
  • Recognised by the club with a Certificate of Appreciation for outstanding dedication and loyalty.

AIUB Cyber Gaming Fest 2022

AIUB Computer Club · AIUB, Dhaka · 1–4 December 2022

  • A four-day esports festival organised by the AIUB Computer Club and powered by Gigabyte, with tournaments in Dota 2, Valorant, FIFA 22, NFS Most Wanted, eFootball and Mobile Legends.
  • Drew about 1,000 students from 100 educational institutions.
  • What I did: worked on the AIUB Computer Club organising team for the fest and received a Certificate of Appreciation for outstanding performance and valuable contribution to the event.
