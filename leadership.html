Leadership & volunteering

AIUB Computer Club

Certificate of Appreciation

A student technology club at AIUB that runs programming contests, workshops and fests. I was an active member of its organising team, helping run the Software Requirements Analysis & QA Seminars (2020–2022), AIUB Cyber Gaming Fest (2018, 2022), the Samsung Esports Championship (2019) and the JARVIS – Intra ACC Carnival (2021), and received the club’s Certificate of Appreciation for dedication and loyalty.

AIUB Cyber Gaming Fest 2022

AIUB Computer Club · AIUB, Dhaka · 1–4 December 2022

A four-day esports fest run by the AIUB Computer Club and powered by Gigabyte, with tournaments in Dota 2, Valorant, FIFA 22 and more, drawing about 1,000 students from 100 institutions. I worked on the organising team and received a Certificate of Appreciation for outstanding performance and valuable contribution.

Acquiring Competencies for Employment (ACE)

BYLC

A two-week BYLC programme that prepares university students for employment. I completed it, covering professional writing (emails, resumes and cover letters), communication and public speaking (including interview etiquette) and leadership training (conscious leadership and teamwork).

Bangladesh Youth Volunteer Award programme

Go Up Foundation and Amra Scout Group · Dhaka · 11 March 2023

A volunteer programme organised by Go Up Foundation with Amra Scout Group, a Dhaka scout group of Bangladesh Scouts that supports street children and runs relief work. I took part in Dhaka on 11 March 2023 and received a certificate of participation.

BD Clean Movement

Volunteer · 29 March 2019

A nationwide volunteer platform, founded in 2016, that cleans a chosen public place every week and had more than 44,000 active members by 2023. I joined a clean-up as a volunteer on 29 March 2019 and was recognised for contributing time, effort and dedication towards a cleaner, greener Bangladesh.
