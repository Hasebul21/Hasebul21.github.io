Hasebul Hassan Chowdhury

Software engineer · problem solver · mentor

I am a software engineer from Bangladesh who builds reliable systems by day and studies applied AI. I turned a love of contest puzzles into a career building software, and I enjoy building useful things as much as helping others learn to build them. For more than four years I have helped product teams, mostly in Norway, build and run their software, and I have co-authored three applied machine learning research papers.

Why software engineering

Competitive programming pulled me in. At AIUB I solved contest problems, more than 2,100 of them over the years, and competed with my team in the Asia Dhaka Regional preliminary contests, which made me love problem solving. During my student years I also built software for fellow students, and I was always curious about games and computer hardware: how a machine actually runs the code we write. That curiosity reached the AIUB Computer Club too, which recognised my contribution to its Cyber Gaming Fest in 2022.

Later, the rise of AI made the field even more interesting. Today I build AI-assisted tools into my own engineering work, and I want to understand how AI agents can help run real systems safely.

What I care about

  • Cloud: building and running software on cloud platforms in a way that stays simple, secure and affordable.
  • Site reliability engineering: keeping systems fast and available, and learning from every incident so it does not happen twice.
  • Cloud agents: AI agents that help people run cloud and software systems safely, with a person in control of risky steps.
  • Data privacy: handling people’s data carefully, collecting only what is needed and keeping it protected.
  • Teaching and mentoring: I mentored juniors in data structures and algorithms at AIUB (2019–2021), and I have reviewed and guided newer engineers’ work at Cefalo.
  • Assistive technology: AI that makes everyday devices, such as hearing aids, adapt to their surroundings on their own.
